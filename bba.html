Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) or equivalent examination from a recognized board in any stream.

There is no minimum percentage requirement for admission.

For detailed eligibility information, contact our admission counsellors.

Payment Modes

UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), Demand Draft, No-Cost EMI through partnered institutions.

All payments are secured and receipted. Fee refund and cancellation applies as per university policy.
