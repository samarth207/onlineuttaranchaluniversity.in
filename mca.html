Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have completed one of the following:

BCA / B.Sc. (Computer Science) / B.Tech (CS/IT) or any equivalent degree from a UGC recognized university.

OR Any graduation with Mathematics as a subject at 10+2 or graduation level.

Minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved category candidates).

For detailed eligibility information, contact our admission counsellors.

Payment Modes

UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), Demand Draft, No-Cost EMI through partnered institutions.

All payments are secured and receipted. Fee refund and cancellation applies as per university policy.
