Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have completed a Bachelor's degree of minimum 3 years duration from a UGC recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved category candidates).

Final year students who are awaiting results may also apply provisionally.

For detailed eligibility information, contact our admission counsellors.

Payment Modes

UPI, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), Demand Draft, No-Cost EMI through partnered institutions.

All payments are secured and receipted. Fee refund and cancellation applies as per university policy.
